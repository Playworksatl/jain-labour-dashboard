The Minimum Wage Ratchet Effect
When minimum wages rise, the entire wage structure shifts — not just entry-level pay. Workers with experience push to restore their differential above the new floor. Supervisors push to maintain their premium. A bid priced at today's wages, covering a contract that spans a scheduled minimum wage increase, carries hidden cost exposure that most BSCs do not model.
